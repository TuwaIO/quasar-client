# verifyPayeeWith()

> **verifyPayeeWith**(`adapters`, `params`): `Promise`\<`boolean` \| `null`\>

Defined in: [nova-payments/src/utils/payee.ts:61](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/payee.ts#L61)

Checks the signature of a quote's payout record with the adapter of its chain family (`verifyPayoutRecord`): the
[PayeeVerifier](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-type-aliases-payeeverifier--docs) of a checkout over its adapters.

Side effects: on EVM, a smart wallet's signature is put to its contract through the app's RPC.

## Parameters

### adapters

readonly [`PaymentsAdapter`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-interfaces-paymentsadapter--docs)[] \| `undefined`

The checkout's payment adapters.

### params

The record, its signature and the network (CAIP-2) of the quote.

`string`

[`PayoutRecord`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-interfaces-payoutrecord--docs)

#### signature

`string`

## Returns

`Promise`\<`boolean` \| `null`\>

Whether the signature holds, or `null` when no adapter of the family checks signatures.
