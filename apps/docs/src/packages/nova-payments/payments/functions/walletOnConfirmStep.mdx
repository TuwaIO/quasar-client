# walletOnConfirmStep()

> **walletOnConfirmStep**(`params`): `"leave"` \| `"keep"` \| `"follow"`

Defined in: [nova-payments/src/utils/checkout.ts:357](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/checkout.ts#L357)

What the active wallet changing does on the step that confirms the wallet, where no new price is locked yet: the step
asks the wallet connected to sign, never the one the store last held.

## Parameters

### params

What the change happens over.

#### open

`boolean`

The step shows.

#### payer

`string` \| `null`

The payer the active wallet gives for the chosen method, or `null`.

#### paysRest

`boolean`

That wallet may pay: only the first wallet pays the rest of an underpaid invoice.

#### storePayer

`string` \| `null`

The payer the store holds, which the step would ask to sign for.

## Returns

`"leave"` \| `"keep"` \| `"follow"`

`keep`: nothing changes; `follow`: this wallet signs, or goes on to the price if it confirmed already; `leave`:
the step closes, with no wallet to ask.
