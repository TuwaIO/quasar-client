# refundTransfer()

> **refundTransfer**(`instructions`): [`PaymentInstructions`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-type-aliases-paymentinstructions--docs) \| `null`

Defined in: [nova-payments/src/utils/refund.ts:20](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/refund.ts#L20)

The transfer of a refund as a payments adapter sends it (`sendTransfer`): the asset of the payment to the payer, on
the chain of the payment. The token is the contract or mint of the CAIP-19 asset, `native` for the coin of the chain.

## Parameters

### instructions

What Quasar asks to send.

#### amount

`string`

Amount in base units.

#### assetId

`string`

CAIP-19 asset.

#### chainId

`string`

CAIP-2 chain.

#### decimals

`number`

Decimals of the token.

#### symbol

`string`

Symbol of the token.

#### to

`string` \| `null`

Recipient: the payer.

## Returns

[`PaymentInstructions`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-type-aliases-paymentinstructions--docs) \| `null`

The transfer, or `null` without a recipient or on a chain no adapter pays on.
