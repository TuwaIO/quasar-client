# checkoutScreen()

> **checkoutScreen**(`state`, `choices?`): [`CheckoutScreen`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-type-aliases-checkoutscreen--docs)

Defined in: [nova-payments/src/utils/checkout.ts:104](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/checkout.ts#L104)

The screen for the state of a checkout store.

## Parameters

### state

The `phase`, `checkout` and `quote` of the store.

#### checkout

[`CheckoutView`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-interfaces-checkoutview--docs) \| `null`

What the checkout token opens; `null` until loaded.

#### error?

[`CheckoutError`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-interfaces-checkouterror--docs) \| `null`

The store's last error: a price refused after the signature shows on the method step.

#### payerProof?

`string`

Whether the connected wallet must confirm the invoice (`payerProof` of the checkout store;
left out by older stores).

#### phase

[`CheckoutPhase`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-type-aliases-checkoutphase--docs)

Where the payment stands.

#### quote?

[`CheckoutQuote`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-interfaces-checkoutquote--docs) \| `null`

The locked price; while the first one is being locked the method screen stays, with its button
busy, so a refused payer or a failed request is shown where the buyer chose (the step that confirms the wallet, when
the price is locked after it).

### choices?

[`CheckoutScreenChoices`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-interfaces-checkoutscreenchoices--docs) = `{}`

What the buyer chose on the page.

## Returns

[`CheckoutScreen`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-type-aliases-checkoutscreen--docs)

The screen: `confirmWallet` once the buyer continued (`choices.confirmWallet`) while the wallet must
confirm the invoice or confirms it, and after it until the price is locked (unless it was refused), also on an
underpaid invoice whose rest another wallet pays, `qr` instead of `pay` when the buyer pays
with a QR code (or the price was locked without a payer), `replaced` for an invoice the seller
corrected, `autoPay` after a paid subscription period while the automatic payments offer is open, `loading` until
the labels of the invoice's language are loaded, `unconfirmed` in the phase of that name of a current
`@tuwaio/quasar-sdk` (Quasar could not confirm the payment, which may still arrive).
