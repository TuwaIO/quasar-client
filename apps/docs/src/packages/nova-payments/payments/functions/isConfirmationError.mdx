# isConfirmationError()

> **isConfirmationError**(`error`): `boolean`

Defined in: [nova-payments/src/utils/payerProof.ts:112](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/payerProof.ts#L112)

Whether an error belongs on the step that confirms the wallet. The store's error may be about something else: a
transfer held from another wallet, or a price refused to the last wallet, opened the step with "Something went wrong"
before the wallet was asked anything.

## Parameters

### error

[`CheckoutError`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-interfaces-checkouterror--docs) \| `null` \| `undefined`

The checkout store's error, if any.

## Returns

`boolean`

`true` for a refusal of the confirmation or a failure of its requests.
