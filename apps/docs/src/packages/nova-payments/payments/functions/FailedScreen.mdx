# FailedScreen()

> **FailedScreen**(): `Element` \| `null`

Defined in: [nova-payments/src/components/FailedScreen.tsx:31](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/components/FailedScreen.tsx#L31)

The failed step: a payment this page showed on its way failed for good (the network reverted it, the wallet
cancelled it, a Solana transaction expired), and the invoice still takes a payment. It stays where the confirm step
was, with the same head, so the buyer sees what became of the payment they were watching: the mark and the chip turn
to a failure, the last step says "Failed", and under the network, the transaction with its explorer link and the
sender stands why, in the words of the reason; of a transaction that ran out of the gas its wallet gave it, also what
gas limit to set before paying again. "Pay again" goes on to the step the checkout store went back to (the locked
price while it holds, else the methods); "Contact" reaches the seller.

Side effects: the chain adapter's reads of a transaction that reverted (`outOfGas`), once per transaction.

## Returns

`Element` \| `null`

The failed step, or nothing without the failure of a payment.
