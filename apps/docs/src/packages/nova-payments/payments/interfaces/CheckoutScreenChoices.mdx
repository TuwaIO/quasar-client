Defined in: [nova-payments/src/utils/checkout.ts:65](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/checkout.ts#L65)

A payment this page followed has just failed for good, and the buyer has not gone on yet: the `failed` step shows
what became of it, in place of the step the store went back to, until the buyer chooses to pay again.

***

Defined in: [nova-payments/src/utils/checkout.ts:60](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/checkout.ts#L60)

The buyer continued with a wallet that must confirm the invoice before the price (`payerProof` of the checkout
store is `required`): the step that confirms it shows while the store waits for the signature and locks the price
after it.

***
