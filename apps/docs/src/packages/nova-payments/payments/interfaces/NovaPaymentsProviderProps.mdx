### adapters?

> `optional` **adapters?**: [`PaymentsAdapter`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-interfaces-paymentsadapter--docs)[]

Defined in: [nova-payments/src/providers/NovaPaymentsProvider.tsx:101](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/providers/NovaPaymentsProvider.tsx#L101)

The chain families the checkouts pay from the app's connected wallet on: `paymentsEVMAdapter` of
`@tuwaio/nova-payments/evm` (on the wagmi config of the app's Satellite EVM adapter), `paymentsSolanaAdapter` of
`@tuwaio/nova-payments/solana`. Without the adapter of a method's family, the buyer pays that method with a QR
code. When Nova uses its own connection, the `adapters` of `ownConnection` replace them. Without `adapters`, a
provider on the connection of a `NovaPaymentsProvider` above (the app's, or Nova's own) uses the adapters of that
provider; `[]` asks for QR codes only.

### ownConnection?

The wallet connection Nova brings when the app has none: used only when no `SatelliteConnectProvider` is above
(see [NovaPaymentsOwnConnection](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-interfaces-novapaymentsownconnection--docs)). Inside the app's Satellite store, Nova uses the app's connection and never

### pulsar?

> `optional` **pulsar?**: [`PaymentsPulsar`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-interfaces-paymentspulsar--docs)

Defined in: [nova-payments/src/providers/NovaPaymentsProvider.tsx:108](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/providers/NovaPaymentsProvider.tsx#L108)

The app's Pulsar store (`injectExternalPendingTxs` of `createPulsarStore` from `@tuwaio/pulsar-core`). Every payment
of a checkout goes to it, which tracks it and keeps it in the history, and Nova Transactions shows its toast: one the
wallet sends at once, one the page did not send (a QR code, a relayed signature) once Quasar records it, with its
hash.
