### recheck

> **recheck**: () => `Promise`\<`boolean`\>

Defined in: [checkout/refundStore.ts:77](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/quasar-sdk/src/checkout/refundStore.ts#L77)

Reads the refund again before a wallet is asked to send it. A window can stay open while the refund is withdrawn or sent
elsewhere, and a wallet asked then would pay out money nobody asks for any more. What it finds shows in `phase`.

Side effects: one `get`; follows a refund it finds submitted.

#### Returns

`Promise`\<`boolean`\>

Whether a wallet may send the refund now: `false` when Quasar does not answer (`error` says so), as it cannot be
known then, and while `sentTransaction` holds a transaction already sent for it.

### start

Defined in: [checkout/refundStore.ts:60](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/quasar-sdk/src/checkout/refundStore.ts#L60)

Asks for a refund of an invoice through `request`. When a transaction sent for an earlier refund of the invoice was
not handed over, that refund comes back instead (`sentTransaction`), or is followed when Quasar took it after all, and
nothing new is asked for while Quasar does not say what became of it.

#### Parameters

##### invoiceId

`string`

The paid or held invoice.

##### params?

[`RefundParams`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-server-interfaces-refundparams--docs)

Amount (everything left when omitted), mode and reason.

#### Returns

`Promise`\<`boolean`\>

Whether it was asked for or came back; `phase` is then `awaitingTransfer` (`confirming` when taken).

### submit

> **submit**: (`params`) => `Promise`\<`boolean`\>

Defined in: [checkout/refundStore.ts:86](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/quasar-sdk/src/checkout/refundStore.ts#L86)

Hands over the refund transaction through `submit`, then follows the refund through `get` until it is final. The
transaction stays in `sentTransaction`, and in [RefundStoreOptions.handOverStorage](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-interfaces-refundstoreoptions--docs#handoverstorage), until Quasar takes it or
refuses it for good: a refusal of 4xx, other than one that says nothing about it (401, 403, 408, 425, 429).

#### Parameters

##### params

[`SubmitRefundParams`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-server-interfaces-submitrefundparams--docs)

The transaction and the merchant wallet it was sent from.

#### Returns

`Promise`\<`boolean`\>

Whether it was taken; `phase` is then `confirming`.
