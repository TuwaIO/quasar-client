### handOverStorage?

> `optional` **handOverStorage?**: [`CheckoutHandOverStorage`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-interfaces-checkouthandoverstorage--docs) \| `null`

Defined in: [checkout/store.ts:526](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/quasar-sdk/src/checkout/store.ts#L526)

Where the store keeps the hand-overs of transactions the wallet sent that Quasar has not taken yet
([CheckoutState.submit](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-interfaces-checkoutstate--docs#submit)), so that the next [CheckoutState.load](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-interfaces-checkoutstate--docs#load) of the same checkout hands them over
again. Defaults to the browser's `sessionStorage` (the tab, until it closes), or none with your own `api`; `null`
keeps nothing. The entry is `quasar-sdk:checkoutHandOvers:<token>`: a JSON array of
`{ txKey, from?, connectorType?, at }` (the transaction, the sending wallet and the wallet connector as passed to
`submit`, and when the store first got it, in milliseconds since 1970), removed once Quasar takes or refuses it.
Failures of the storage are ignored.

***
