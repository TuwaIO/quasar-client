Whether a quote of the method goes to an address of the invoice's own (ERC-5564): it needs no paying wallet, so the

### payerProof?

> `optional` **payerProof?**: [`PayerProofNeed`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-type-aliases-payerproofneed--docs)

Defined in: [checkout/types.ts:333](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/quasar-sdk/src/checkout/types.ts#L333)

When a payment with the method needs the paying wallet's confirmation of the invoice
([CheckoutState.confirmPayer](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-interfaces-checkoutstate--docs#confirmpayer)): `before_quote` (an EVM method: before the price; the quote answers
`409 payer_proof_required` without it), `unless_relayed` (an EVM method the gasless relay may pay: the price comes
first, the relay's EIP-3009 authorization confirms the wallet, and a transfer or a paymaster payment of it needs
the confirmation first), `none` (Solana: the quote's reference ties the transfer to the invoice). Quasar versions
before October 29, 2026 leave it out: no confirmation.
