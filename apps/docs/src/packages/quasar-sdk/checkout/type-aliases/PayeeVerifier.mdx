# PayeeVerifier

> **PayeeVerifier** = (`params`) => `Promise`\<`boolean` \| `null`\>

Defined in: [checkout/payee.ts:96](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/quasar-sdk/src/checkout/payee.ts#L96)

Checks the signature of a payout record by its own address: the chain adapters of `@tuwaio/nova-payments` do it on
EVM (an EOA by recovery, a smart wallet through its contract on `chainId`, ERC-1271 or ERC-6492) and on Solana
(Ed25519).

## Parameters

### params

The record, its signature and the network (CAIP-2) of the quote.

#### chainId

`string`

#### record

[`PayoutRecord`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-interfaces-payoutrecord--docs)

#### signature

`string`

## Returns

`Promise`\<`boolean` \| `null`\>

Whether the signature is the record's address's, or `null` when this page cannot check signatures of the
  network's family (no chain adapter for it): the record is matched, its signature not checked (`signed: false`).
