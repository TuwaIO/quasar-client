# checkQuotePayee()

> **checkQuotePayee**(`quote`, `options?`): `Promise`\<[`PayeeCheckResult`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-type-aliases-payeecheckresult--docs)\>

Defined in: [checkout/payee.ts:299](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/quasar-sdk/src/checkout/payee.ts#L299)

Checks the payee of a quote before the page shows its address or asks a wallet for anything: every place of the
address names the address of the instructions (`payee_mismatch`), the merchant's pinned addresses include it
(`payee_not_pinned`), and the payout record covers its network and address and is signed by it (`verify`;
`payee_unverified`). A quote with `payee: null` has no record (`payee_unsigned`). A quote without the field comes
from an engine older than payout records: its places and the pinned addresses are still checked (`signed: false`).

A quote to an invoice's own address (`invoiceAddress`) is checked against its meta-address record instead: an EVM
record of version 2 that names the quote's network (`payee_mismatch`), signed by a payout wallet among the pinned
addresses (`payee_not_pinned`), and an address that comes from the record's meta-address and the one-time key, with
the record's signature its wallet's (`verifyInvoiceAddress`; `payee_unverified`).

## Returns

`Promise`\<[`PayeeCheckResult`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-checkout-type-aliases-payeecheckresult--docs)\>

What the check found. A verifier that throws counts as `payee_unverified`; one that answers `null` (it
  cannot check the network's family) leaves the signature unchecked (`signed: false`).
