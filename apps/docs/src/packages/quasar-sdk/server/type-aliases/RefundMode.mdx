# RefundMode

> **RefundMode** = `"fiat_value"` \| `"exact_received"` \| `"excess"`

Defined in: [modules/payments/types.ts:623](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/quasar-sdk/src/modules/payments/types.ts#L623)

How a refund is priced: at today's price of the invoice amount (`fiat_value`), the whole payment as received
(`exact_received`), or the overpaid excess alone, as received (`excess`: no credit note, no refunded total, the invoice
keeps its status).
