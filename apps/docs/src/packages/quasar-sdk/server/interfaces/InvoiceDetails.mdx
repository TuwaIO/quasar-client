# InvoiceDetails

Defined in: [modules/payments/types.ts:472](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/quasar-sdk/src/modules/payments/types.ts#L472)

An invoice with its newest refunds and how many refunds and credit notes it has, as
[PaymentsModule.getInvoice](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-server-classes-paymentsmodule--docs#getinvoice) returns it. It also names what happened to the money, oldest first. Every refund and correction adds a credit note, so the full lists are
[PaymentsModule.listInvoiceRefunds](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-server-classes-paymentsmodule--docs#listinvoicerefunds) and [PaymentsModule.listInvoiceDocuments](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-server-classes-paymentsmodule--docs#listinvoicedocuments).

### reviewReason

> **reviewReason**: `string` \| `null`

Defined in: [modules/payments/types.ts:353](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/quasar-sdk/src/modules/payments/types.ts#L353)

Why the payment waits for you: `overpaid` when the app flags overpayments, `quote_expired` when it arrived after
its locked quote expired, `payment_reverted` when a payment Quasar credited is gone from the chain (check the
transaction `payment.reverted` names; the invoice keeps its status), `refund_reverted` when a confirmed refund of its
payment is gone from the chain (`refund.reverted` names it; the refund and its credit note stay, and Quasar takes no
second refund for it: send the money again outside it), `unverified_arrival` when a contract sent coin to the
invoice's own address inside its own call (an exchange withdrawal, a Safe, a smart account), which Quasar cannot
confirm and which names no sender: check the address in a block explorer and accept it; no refund goes back through
Quasar without a sender. A transfer Quasar could tie to no invoice never puts the invoice under review: it is an
unmatched transfer of its own.
