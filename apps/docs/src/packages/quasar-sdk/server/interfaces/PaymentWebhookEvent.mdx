# PaymentWebhookEvent

Defined in: [webhooks.ts:113](https://github.com/TuwaIO/quasar-client/blob/main/packages/quasar-sdk/src/webhooks.ts#L113)

The body of a payment webhook (`payments.v1`). `id`, `type` and `createdAt` are those of the ledger event, so
deduplicate by `id`; `data.invoice` is the invoice when the delivery was sent, so a retry carries its current state.
Deliveries keep no order of their own: order the events of an invoice by `seq`.

#### mightHaveLanded?

> `optional` **mightHaveLanded?**: `true`

Nothing proves that the money stayed: `invoice.expired` of an invoice whose gasless or sponsored payment was still
on its way, `refund.failed` of a refund whose transaction may still land. Check before selling the goods on or
paying again.

#### payments?

> `optional` **payments?**: [`InvoicePayment`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-server-interfaces-invoicepayment--docs)[]

Every transfer credited to the invoice so far, on `invoice.paid`, `invoice.underpaid`, `invoice.needs_review`
and `invoice.payment_accepted`: an invoice paid in parts has several.

#### refund?

> `optional` **refund?**: [`Refund`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-server-interfaces-refund--docs)

The refund of a refund event. One of mode `excess` sends back the overpaid excess alone: no
`invoice.refunded`, `invoice.partially_refunded` or credit note follows it.

#### review?

> `optional` **review?**: [`PaymentWebhookReview`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-server-interfaces-paymentwebhookreview--docs)

#### settlement?

> `optional` **settlement?**: [`InvoiceSettlement`](?path=/docs/packages-quasar-sdk-server-interfaces-invoicesettlement--docs)

The payment, once the invoice has one.

#### transaction?

> `optional` **transaction?**: `object`

The transaction to check, in `payment.reverted`, `refund.reverted` (a confirmed refund the network dropped: the
refund stays, and Quasar takes no second refund for it; a refund of the invoice's payment keeps its credit note and
the invoice waits for review with `reviewReason` `refund_reverted`, while a refund of an unmatched transfer, named
in `refund.unmatchedTransfer`, has no credit note and marks no invoice) and `payment.unverified`.

The transfer of `invoice.transfer_unmatched` and `invoice.transfer_unmatched_voided`. The refund of an unmatched
